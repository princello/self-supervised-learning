Pulls complete. This trade-off — balancing exploration against exploitation — is the heart of RL.

Did you feel it? One machine pays out and starts to feel lucky. You want to pull it forever. But what if the other one is secretly better? That tug-of-war in your head is the whole game.

That tug-of-war has a name: the explore/exploit dilemma. Good strategies balance checking new options against milking the best-known one. This trade-off sits at the heart of every reinforcement learning system.
